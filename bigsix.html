Behaviour risk factors

The "Big Six" Financial Traps

Identify the psychological vulnerabilities that trigger compound debt.

🧩 STATUS ANXIETY

Impression Spending

Purchasing luxury goods or high-end experiences to project wealth, seek social approval, and boost standing.

🐍 PRESENT BIAS

Consumer Credit Creep

Normalizing high-interest micro-debts, credit limits, and Buy-Now-Pay-Later loops that slowly erode monthly cash flow.

🦩 OSTRICH EFFECT

Financial Fog

Deliberately avoiding checking bank accounts and debt balances to escape short-term financial anxiety.

🦊 HEDONIC TREADMILL

Lifestyle Inflation

Automatically scaling discretionary spending upward with every income increase, preventing wealth accumulation.

🐦 EMOTIONAL SPENDING

Impulse Dopamine Loops

Utilizing retail therapy and instant purchases as a primary coping mechanism for stress, boredom, or sadness.

🦗 OPTIMISM BIAS

Zero-Safety Net Living

Operating month-to-month with minimal liquid reserves, assuming current earnings will remain uninterrupted.
